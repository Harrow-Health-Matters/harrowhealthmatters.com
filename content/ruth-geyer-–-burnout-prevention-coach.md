After two decades in the corporate world and my own experience of burnout, I now support individuals in preventing burnout, building resilience, and finding a more sustainable way of living and working.\
\
My approach combines practical, evidence-informed techniques with compassionate coaching. Through breathwork, nervous system regulation, mindfulness, and mindset coaching, I help people better understand their stress responses, reconnect with themselves, and develop the tools to navigate life's challenges with greater calm, clarity, and confidence.\
\
I believe that lasting change doesn't come from pushing harder, it comes from understanding ourselves better and making small, consistent changes that support our wellbeing. My aim is to create a safe, supportive space where people feel seen, heard, and empowered to move forward with renewed strength. \

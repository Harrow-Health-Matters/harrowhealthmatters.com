Sweta Purohit is the founder of The Sweta Way MMT Framework, helping people who feel stuck or at life’s crossroads heal and overcome challenges, ignite their inner wisdom and creative solutions, and lead with confidence, soul-aligned direction and purposeful vision through an integrated mind, body and energy approach.

Her unique approach integrates the subconscious mind, somatic body and energy together in real time for real shifts, recognising that lasting transformation happens when all three work together. By combining subconscious transformation, somatic embodiment and energy healing, Sweta helps people regulate their nervous system, transform unconscious patterns, release emotional and energetic blocks, and unlock fresh perspectives and creative solutions that create meaningful, lasting change.

With over 15 years’ experience in leadership development, transformational change and holistic healing, Sweta is an Accredited Superconscious Coach, Qualified Somatic Embodiment Facilitator and Energy Alchemy Healer. Her work bridges practical leadership with ancient wisdom, drawing inspiration from creative life force energy, where Shakti represents transformation and Shiva provides the grounded structure and conscious direction that allows that energy to be expressed. Together with the subconscious mind and somatic body, these principles form the foundation of her integrated approach.

Sweta works with individuals through transformational coaching, somatic embodiment and energy healing, and delivers workshops, talks and holistic wellbeing programmes for community groups and professionals. She is passionate about helping people reconnect with their inner wisdom and soul-body connection so they can create lasting transformation from the inside out.
